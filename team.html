Team

INVESTMENT  BANKING
PCF Investment Banking team brings together savvy and reliable financial experts, highly seasoned in the Romanian financial landscape, with a common set of values, rooted in integrity and excellence.

Our collective experience covers everything from market fundamentals and financial analysis to overseeing the entire transaction process.

Our in-depth knowledge of the market gives us a unique vantage point in all the stages of a transaction, to the benefit of our clients.

Leadership

Mihai Iordan

Founder & Managing Partner

Mihai Iordan is the Founder and Managing Partner of PCF Investment Banking. With over 20 years in corporate finance, he advises shareholders and C-suite executives on structured finance, debt advisory, private equity and M&A transactions, drawing on an extensive network of banks, investors and corporate leaders across Romania and the CEE region.
Mihai Iordan is the Founder of PCF Investment Banking, bringing deep transactional expertise across structured finance, debt advisory, private equity, and M&A. He provides strategic counsel to C-suite executives and shareholders on complex, high-stakes transactions, backed by an in-depth understanding of the Romanian financial ecosystem. He maintains an extensive institutional network spanning private equity funds, banks, and corporate leaders across Romania and the wider CEE region. Prior to founding PCF, Mihai held key corporate banking positions, including Large Corporate Banker at UniCredit, Deputy Head of Corporate at Libra Bank, having built his foundation at Ernst & Young. Further, he held management positions at Schnecker Van Wyk & Pearson Romania and was an Equity Partner at Oxygen.

Transaction Team

Vlad Florea

Director, Investment Banking

Vlad Florea is Director, Investment Banking at PCF and leads the execution of the firm’s syndicated and club loan mandates. He joined in 2022 after serving as Senior Banker in BCR’s Large Corporate Agribusiness & Food division and as CFO of Cerealcom Dolj Group, bringing both the lender’s and the borrower’s perspective to every transaction.
Vlad Florea joined PCF Investment Banking in 2022 as Director, Investment Banking, and leads the execution of the firm’s syndicated and club loan mandates. He brings a combined banking and corporate perspective, having worked on both sides of the financing table.

Before joining PCF, Vlad was Senior Banker in BCR’s Large Corporate Agribusiness & Food division. Earlier, he served as Chief Financial Officer of Cerealcom Dolj Group, one of Romania’s largest agricultural producers and traders, where he led the financial, accounting and reporting functions and was a key contributor to the group doubling its volumes and turnover over three years. Vlad graduated from the Faculty of Finance, Insurance, Banking and Stock Exchange and holds an MBA from the Bucharest University of Economic Studies.

Teodor Banu

Director, Investment Banking

Teodor Banu is Director, Investment Banking at PCF, with 20 years of experience in corporate banking and risk management. He joined in 2025 from OTP Bank Romania, where, as Corporate Underwriting Director, he oversaw the structuring and approval of large corporate and project finance loans across agribusiness, construction, manufacturing and real estate.
Teodor Banu joined PCF Investment Banking in 2025 as Director, Investment Banking. He previously served as Corporate Underwriting Director at OTP Bank Romania, where he oversaw the structuring and approval of a significant number of large corporate and project finance loans across agribusiness, construction, manufacturing and real estate.

With 20 years of experience in corporate banking and risk management, Teodor has developed tailored financing solutions, from syndicated loans to credit arrangements and risk management services, helping companies grow on a sound financial footing. He brings clarity and structure to complex transactions, making sure every stakeholder understands each step and that execution remains efficient. Teodor graduated from the Faculty of Finance, Insurance, Banking and Stock Exchange at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies and holds the ACCA qualification.

Manager

Adrian Tudor rejoined PCF Investment Banking in 2024 as Manager. He was first part of the PCF team for two years before taking on the role of Chief Financial Officer at a real estate group, where he strengthened his leadership skills and broadened his financial expertise.
