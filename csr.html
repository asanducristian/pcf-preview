INVESTMENT BANKING

Corporate
social responsibility

Beyond the transactions, PCF Investment Banking supports a small group of Romanian organisations working with children, patients, people with disabilities and animals in need. We choose partners we can stay with over time, and whose work we can see directly.

Asociația Păsări

Asociația Păsări funds the education of children living with serious illness. Through monthly scholarships it covers therapy, tutoring and learning materials, so that a child in treatment does not fall behind and can return to the classroom afterwards. The association currently supports 100 children — filling a gap the public system leaves open.

Fundația Renașterea

Founded in 2001, Fundația Renașterea is Romania's best-known women's health foundation. It runs medical screening centres in Bucharest and mobile diagnostic units that reach rural communities where screening would otherwise not exist. Across 25 years it has carried out more than 132,000 medical investigations, provided over 42,700 free Pap smears and mammograms, and donated more than 2,200 wigs to women in cancer treatment.

Asociația Angel Dog

Based in Satu Mare, Angel Dog trains guide dogs for people with visual impairments and runs animal-assisted therapy programmes for people living with disabilities. Its work gives people back a degree of independence that no device can replace.

Asociația De profesie, Om

Based in Băicoi, Prahova, De profesie, Om supports children with special needs and developmental disorders, and people facing serious illness. It runs entirely on donations and volunteers, on a simple principle: where there is a person in need, they will not be left alone.

Asociația Sache

Founded in 2007 in Tărtășești, Dâmbovița, Sache runs Romania's first social veterinary hospital. It treats around 2,600 animals a year regardless of whether they have an owner, operates two mobile clinics, and has carried out over 115,000 sterilisations. Its EduSache programme has reached 2,500 children in 38 schools. Every animal it takes in is treated until it has fully recovered.
